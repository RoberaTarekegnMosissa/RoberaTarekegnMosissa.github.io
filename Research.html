MSc Thesis Research

Diagnosing Hydrological-Cycle Biases in State-of-the-Art CMIP6 Climate Models over the Congo Basin

My MSc research evaluates how CMIP6 climate models represent precipitation, evaporation, seasonal variability, and atmospheric moisture transport over the Congo Basin.

The project combines climate-model simulations, reanalysis datasets, observation-based products, and reproducible Python workflows. Additional results and research outputs will be added as the project progresses.

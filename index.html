I am an MSc student in Water Resources Engineering at KU Leuven and Vrije Universiteit Brussel. My research interests lie at the intersection of hydrology, climate science, and water resources management.

My current research examines hydrological-cycle biases in CMIP6 climate models over the Congo Basin. I use climate models, reanalysis products, and reproducible Python workflows to study precipitation, evaporation, and hydroclimatic variability.

I am particularly interested in flood risk, climate-model evaluation, and hydroclimatic processes in African river basins and other data-scarce regions.
